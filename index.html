✦ THE PHILOSOPHY

A PLACE TO
SLOW DOWN.

Set along the tranquil waters of the Tungabhadra Canal in Basapur, Maya Valley Resort is crafted as an intimate sanctuary where ancient granite boulders and whispering coconut palms shape your quietest moments.

Here, luxury is defined not by hurry, but by stillness — waking to birdsong over the canal, resting beneath handcrafted thatch breezeways, and letting the timeless rhythms of Hampi take over.

✦ OPEN-AIR DINING

MEALS BENEATH
THATCHED ROOFS.

Dine in spacious open pavilions constructed from natural bamboo, local granite stone, and high thatched ceilings. Designed to channel the evening breeze off the water while you savor wholesome local flavors.

From morning South Indian filter coffee watching the mist rise over the canal to peaceful twilight dinners under warm brass lanterns, dining at Maya Valley is an unhurried communal pleasure.

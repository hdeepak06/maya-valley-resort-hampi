01 · ABOUT THE RETREAT

A sanctuary crafted between stone, water and silence.

Tucked along the historic Tungabhadra Left Canal in Basapur, Maya Valley Resort was envisioned as an unhurried haven where travelers can embrace the ancient majesty of Hampi without compromising on peaceful luxury.

Surrounded by coconut palms, natural stone courtyards, and scenic boulder silhouettes, our resort offers a soothing pause after long days of exploring 14th-century Vijayanagara ruins. Here, mornings awaken to cool canal breezes and nights close beneath starlit Deccan skies.

Natural Stone Heritage

Earthy architecture inspired by the timeless granite boulders of the Hampi valley.

Canal-Side Tranquility

Refreshing breezes and lush gardens away from heavy highway traffic and crowd noise.

Golden Hour Sunsets

Unmatched sunset vistas painting the ancient Hampi ridges in amber and terracotta.

Central Heritage Proximity

Convenient access to Virupaksha Temple, Sanapur Lake, and the Vittala Complex.

06 · HAMPI HIGHLIGHTS

Nearby Heritage & Natural Wonders

Key landmarks easily reachable from Maya Valley Resort. Click "View on Map" on any card to locate it instantly.

SACRED TEMPLE
📍 ~8 km from resort ⏱ ~16 mins

Virupaksha Temple

The oldest functioning temple in Hampi, dedicated to Lord Shiva, featuring a towering 50-meter eastern gopuram overlooking Hampi Bazaar.

UNESCO MONUMENT
📍 ~10 km from resort ⏱ ~20 mins

Vijaya Vittala & Stone Chariot

The crowning jewel of Vijayanagara architecture, world-renowned for its musical stone pillars and the iconic monolithic granite Garuda chariot.

HISTORIC BAZAAR
📍 ~8 km from resort ⏱ ~16 mins

Hampi Bazaar

A kilometer-long historic street flanked by ancient two-storey stone pavilions that once bustled with ruby, silk, and diamond merchants.

SUNSET HILL
📍 ~8 km from resort ⏱ ~15 mins

Hemakuta Hill

A gently sloping granite hill studded with pre-Vijayanagara shrines, world-famous for its magical golden hour sunset vistas over Virupaksha.

SUNRISE SUMMIT
📍 ~8.5 km from resort ⏱ ~18 mins

Matanga Hill

The highest vantage point in central Hampi, offering breathtaking 360-degree panoramic sunrise views over temples, rivers, and endless boulder ridges.

SECLUDED VALLEY TEMPLE
📍 ~9 km from resort ⏱ ~18 mins

Achyutaraya Temple

Secluded in a quiet valley behind Matanga Hill, this atmospheric 16th-century temple complex retains profound tranquility and intact stone artistry.

RIVER & CORACLE BOATS
📍 ~8 km from resort ⏱ ~16 mins

Tungabhadra River Viewpoints

The sacred lifeblood of Hampi, cutting through granite boulder hills where traditional circular coracle boats float past ancient stone ghats.
